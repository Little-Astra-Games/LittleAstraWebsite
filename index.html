What We Do

Godot First

We build all of our games using the Godot game engine. It's open source, powerful, and we believe in its future by building all Little Astra Games' games on it.

Godot

Indie to the Core

We're a small, independent indie game studio making the games we want to play.

Indie

Learn & Grow

We're dedicated to helping people learn the Godot engine and build real skills that lead to real jobs in game development by providing educational material, some open sourced code, and tutorials.

Learn

Develop Job Opportunities

Not only is educational growth a cornerstone of Little Astra Games, we are also looking to grow the job market for Godot developers, artists, composers, etc. The current Godot job market is limited to freelancers and the growth of development studios like ours being Godot first will help drive that demand.
